What we manufacture

Six product lines built in the same workshop, to the same standard — so a jacket order and a belt order leave the floor looking like they came off one bench.

Jackets

Biker, bomber, trucker and blazer silhouettes in cowhide, lambskin and nappa. Quilted, fleece or nylon linings, with zips and hardware to your spec.

Gloves

Driving, dress and work gloves, lined or unlined, cut to the sizing chart you supply.

Wallets

Bifold, trifold and card wallets, with optional RFID-blocking lining and blind or foil embossing.

Bags

Totes, messenger bags, duffels and backpacks, built on the same pattern-and-stitch line as the jackets.

Belts

Dress and casual belts in single or double-layer hide, with brass, nickel or antique buckles.

Pants

Leather trousers and chaps, straight or slim cut, reinforced at the stress seams.

How a hide becomes a garment

Every order — one jacket or several hundred — moves through the same five stages before it's packed.

01

Sourcing & grading

Hides are graded by hand for grain, thickness and flaw before they're approved for cutting.

02

Pattern cutting

Every panel is cut to your spec sheet or an approved sample, grain direction checked panel by panel.

03

Skiving & stitching

Edges are skived for a flat seam, then saddle-stitched or lock-stitched depending on the piece.

04

Hardware & finishing

Zips, buckles, rivets and linings are set by hand; edges are burnished and sealed.

05

Inspection & packing

Every unit is checked against the approved sample before it's packed for export.
